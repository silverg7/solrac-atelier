03 / EXECUTIVE PROTOCOL

The 4-Phase Tectonic Protocol

Every SOLRAC commission adheres to a strict engineering protocol, from initial quarry block selection to final structural certification.

01

Geotechnical Study & Block Selection

Thorough site topography survey, earth thrust calculation, and individual inspection of granite quarry blocks for mineral density and zero micro-fractures.

02

Millimetric Dressing & Squaring

Geometric dressing and custom surface texturing (bush-hammered, flamed, or hand-pointed) in strict accordance with the architectural blueprint.

03

Structural Interlocking & Concealed Drainage

Interlocking course assembly with mechanical bonding, subsurface hydrological relief piping, and marine-grade stainless anchors where specified.

04

Technical Direction & Certification

Plumb-line laser verification, drainage flow testing, and delivery of technical liability documentation assuring multi-generational longevity.
